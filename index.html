Controls
Joystick (bottom left)Push the way you want to go: a little = walk, further = run, edge = sprint. Let go and he's back on automatic. It also aims the button
Action button (bottom right)Changes with the situation, always in the joystick direction. TACKLE when they have it. CALL when a team-mate has it. With the ball: a quick tap PASSES to the team-mate you're aiming at (green ring), or into space; out on the wing in their half it CROSSES into the box automatically. Press and hold to SHOOT: the longer the hold, the harder the shot
Hold and drag (prototype)Put your finger down, hold a moment, then drag: a dotted line shows where you'll run. Short = walk, longer = run, longest = sprint. Let go and he keeps going that way until you change it. Works with or without the ball
With the ballTap a team-mate to pass to him: the game picks the right ball (short pass, through ball into his run, long ball or cross). Double-tap him to chip it. Tap the pitch to dribble that way; double-tap to sprint with it
TimingA green arrow shows a team-mate making a forward run. Pass as he runs, onside, through an open lane, for a well-timed ball
Tap yourselfCall for the ball: he passes it and you go and get it. If it's loose, you go for it
Flick towards goalShoot. Faster flick = harder and higher; bend it to curl. When a cross arrives, flick to head it
Tap an opponentTrack him (red circle) until your team wins it back. Double-tap to track at a sprint
Swipe without the ballTackle in the direction you swipe: close in it's a stabbing tackle, from a few yards a slide. Point it at the ball. From behind, or a lunge, risks a card; stop a clear run on goal and it's red
Tap empty pitchMake a run there (double-tap to sprint)

Your attributes matter: pace decides who wins a race, passing how often the ball goes astray, shooting your accuracy, dribbling your first touch and how hard you are to tackle, stamina how long your legs last.
